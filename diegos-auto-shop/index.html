Our Shop

Diego's Auto Shop is a local, family-owned auto repair business located at 911 Rio Grande St in Salt Lake City's Granary District, serving the Peoples Freeway and Ballpark neighborhoods.

We focus on honest diagnostics, transparent pricing, and clear communication — so you always know exactly what your vehicle needs before any work begins. Our team, led by manager Angel, is proud to be a trusted, growing shop in the community.

We're happy to serve both English and Spanish-speaking customers — se habla español.
