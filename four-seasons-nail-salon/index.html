About Us

Four Seasons Nail Salon is a full-service nail salon located at 464 S 600 E, Unit B, in the Central City-Liberty Wells neighborhood of Salt Lake City.

We offer pedicures, manicures, nail art and designs, gel, acrylic, pink & white sets, and waxing in a welcoming, relaxed atmosphere. Walk-ins are always welcome, and appointments can be made by phone.
