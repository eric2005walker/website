Our Story

The Dollar Barber Shop was founded in 1964 by Howard D. Hinckley, who charged just $1 per haircut and believed that “Quality Sells Itself.” He ran the shop six days a week for nearly 40 years, greeting every customer like family.

Today the shop is run by his daughter, Dayna, who has kept the same friendly, no-frills spirit alive — quality haircuts at a fair price, with little to no wait, for men, women, boys and girls.

Located near 9th & 9th in Salt Lake City, we're proud to still be a neighborhood barber shop where regulars are treated like friends and family.
